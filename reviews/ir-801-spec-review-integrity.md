## Verdict

Changes requested. Behavior and ACs agree on condition order, the three extent states, whole-key read order (matching quire-exact's derived Ord on ObjectReference: universe, object_type, object), the Closed/Partial resolve rules and the row-limit pull order. Two consistency defects: a global 'never read unbounded caller input' statement contradicts the no-count-limit rule for extents, and the Dependencies section asserts agreement with FR-364 condition 3 where FR-364's text says the opposite for absent ends.
