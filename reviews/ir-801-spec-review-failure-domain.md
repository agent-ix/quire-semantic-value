## Verdict

Changes requested. Well covered: the row-limit boundary (0, exact, below), zero-row exhaustion, enumerator error, supertype coverage with a conflicting exact-type entry (AC-14), universe-versus-type equality (AC-8) and refusal precedence across the four FR-364 conditions. Open failure domains: multi-member-type populations cannot be expressed under the duplicate-P rule as stated; the admission unit for a batch of extents is ambiguous against 'refused input publishes nothing'; a Closed manifest is not bound to the environment or closure it attests and Partial's public fields can be forged; limit-before-validation is untested; and two precedence orders are unstated.
